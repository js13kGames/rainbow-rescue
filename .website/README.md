## Story
The unicorns are lost in the forest and want to fly away. Everyone knows rainbows give magic powers. So make your rainbow reach the unicorn to whisk it away!

## Controls
Click and drag on the cloud to make a rainbow. Drag downward. No keyboard controls.

## Gameplay
Draw a rainbow. It repeats the pattern you drew until it hits something. Try to hit the unicorns.

10 levels. Mobile friendly.
